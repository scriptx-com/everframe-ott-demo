## Artwork and video

Everything in `assets/images`, `assets/brand` and `assets/video` was made for
this demo. The posters and stills were generated with an image model from
original prompts; they show no real people, brands, logos or text. The episode
clips in `assets/video` are camera moves over those stills, made with
`scripts/make-clips.sh`. Nocturne TV, its titles, synopses and characters are
fictional.

These assets are covered by the same MIT licence as the code.
